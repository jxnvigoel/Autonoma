# Product Requirements Document (PRD): AeroFleet Hangar

## 1. Overview & Vision
AeroFleet Hangar is an offline-first, high-precision aviation fleet airworthiness management workstation designed for Part 145 aircraft maintenance repair stations. It enables certified technicians and chief inspectors to execute work orders, resolve squawks, audit airworthiness directives, and sign off release-to-service documentation with guaranteed regulatory compliance.

| `[P-02]` | Chief Inspector | Quality & Airworthiness Director | Audits component flight hours, verifies corrective actions, issues FAA Form 8130 release |
